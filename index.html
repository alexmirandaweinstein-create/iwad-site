A new adult contemporary fiction

After a much-needed post-breakup trip, 26-year-old Frankie decides to finally change what’s become a dreary life back home in Los Angeles. She takes her savings and embarks on a solo backpacking trip across Southeast Asia. Or what she begrudgingly deems her Eat, Pray, Love era.

As her adventure begins, she becomes obsessed with the idea of finding her next romance, solving her queerness, and ultimately rediscovering who she is, in an attempt to dislodge her many suppressed emotions. Only to be met with the realities of what she soon discovers as the banana pancake route. A Western-oriented breeding ground that almost swallows her whole as she ventures on this hot, drug-fueled odyssey, documenting it all through a series of detailed travel journals.
