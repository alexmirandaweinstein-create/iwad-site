At twenty-six, Frankie leaves home with a backpack, a one-way ticket, and the belief that somewhere across Southeast Asia, a better version of her is waiting.

Instead, she finds hostel bunk beds, fleeting romances, questionable decisions, and strangers who become harder to leave behind than any place. As the journey unfolds, so does the story Frankie has been telling herself—about why she left, who she loves, and what she is really searching for.

It Wasn’t a Dream is a funny, intimate, and deeply human novel about the people we meet in passing, the stories we create to survive our twenties, and what happens when finding yourself turns out to be the wrong destination.
